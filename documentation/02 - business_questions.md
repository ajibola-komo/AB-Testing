# Business Questions

## Primary Question

Should the organization replace the existing landing page with the new landing page?

## Supporting Questions

- Does the treatment page improve the conversion rate?
- Is the observed improvement statistically significant?
- Is traffic evenly distributed between control and treatment groups?
- Are there any invalid experiment assignments that could bias the results?
- Which countries have the highest conversion rates?
- Does session duration differ between converters and non-converters?
- Are there user behavior patterns that explain conversion performance?

## Expected Business Outcome

Provide product managers with evidence-based recommendations on whether to roll out, reject, or continue testing the new landing page.
